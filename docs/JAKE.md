# Jake: how to work BouldLeads

**Sign in:** open the link Brian sent, name `Jake`, your password. Works on laptop and phone. Bookmark it.
The first time you sign in, a 1-minute welcome tour walks you through the app; reopen it any time from **Guide** in the sidebar.

## Your morning, in order
1. **Call list → Callbacks** (amber "due" badge): people who asked you to call back. Call these first, at the time they gave.
2. **Call list → Replied**: they answered one of our emails. Call them now; they're warm.
3. **Call list → To call**: new leads, best first. The **Personal list** filter (top left) shows the hottest ones (Tier A); call those from your own phone. **Dialer CSV** exports the rest for the dialer.
4. **Call list → Call again**: tried before, emails finished, no answer. Give them another go.

## On each lead card
- **Priority** (left), tier badge, Google rating, and the **local time** where they are (amber = probably closed, call later).
- **Open with:** the #1 problem the audit found. That's your first sentence.
- The green phone button dials (on phone or with your Mac's calling).
- **Full audit & call brief** opens everything: scores, talking points, the 12-point checklist to fill in live (Yes / No), notes, and the activity history.

## After every call, tap one button
| Button | When | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| **No answer** | Nobody picked up | Email 1 goes out today; then day 3, 5 and 12. Stops by itself if they reply. |
| **Voicemail** | You left a message | Same emails as No answer |
| **Call back** | They asked you to call later | Pick the time. Emails stop. They show up in *Callbacks* when it's due. |
| **Booked** | Audit call booked | Emails stop. Lead moves to *Booked & won*. |
| **Not interested** | Clear no | Emails stop. Lead closed. |
| **More → Won** | They became a client 🎉 | |
| **More → Wrong number** | Bad number | Leaves the call list; emails continue if we have an address. |
| **More → Do not call** | They asked us to stop | Calls *and* emails stop forever. |

Made a mistake? **More → Clear status**, then tap the right one. Every tap is logged with your name on the lead's Activity.

## Other pages
- **Places**: live map. Countries shade by leads; zoom in for cities and hot spots; click a place on the right to fly there. "Called" / "Booked" re-colour the map by results.
- **Leads**: the full list with filters (place, search, stage, tier). Select rows to start emails, re-audit or delete. Copy the URL to share a filtered view.
- **Replies**: every email reply, with a Call button.

## Rules of thumb
- Call in their business hours (look at the local time on the card).
- Take notes on the lead page while you talk: Brian sees them instantly.
- Don't email someone by hand who's already in the follow-up: the app does it.
- Questions → Brian.
